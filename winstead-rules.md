# Cyber Incident Response Dashboard

Build a Cyber Incident Response Dashboard designed to act as a "command center" during, and after a Cyber Incident.

## Required Components

1. **Secure and Isolated:** The system should be highly secure and should operate inside potentially compromised networks as an isolated system.
2. **Access Control:** Administrative ability to add individuals to the platform.
3. **Incident Documentation:** Ability to enter details of the incident in a manner that reflects the standards for cyber incident documentation. Account for real-time updating and status monitoring.
4. **Scope Definitions:** Related to incident documentation, but specifically documenting the range of data that has been compromised and the status of the compromised data (locked for ransomware, exfiltrated, etc.).
5. **Real-Time Communications:** Account for real-time communications within the dashboard.

## Judging Criteria

- **Security:** The assumption must be that the attacker has access to all internal systems. The dashboard must be independently secure.
- **Ease of Use:** The team entering the dashboard will be under stress and will not likely recall tutorials. The ability to operate in the dashboard with little instruction is almost as important as security.
- **Creativity:** Anything outside of the described objectives that enhances the functionality of the dashboard.
